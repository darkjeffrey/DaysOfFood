# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- Updated for Bannerlord v1.5.7.
- The launcher no longer warns that the dependencies (Native, Harmony, ButterLib, UIExtenderEx, MCM) don't match: each one now states the version it needs.

## 1.0.0
- The campaign map bar shows how many days your food will last (for example 12d) instead of how many food items you carry.
- The number turns red when food is low and blinks red when it's nearly gone, with an announcement and a map notification at each step.
- Options to show tenths of a day and to show the food amount above the days.
- Mod Configuration Menu options for the thresholds, colors, alerts and debug messages.
